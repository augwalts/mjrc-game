# Inspiration index — artists, designers, works

Curated starting points for the moodboard direction (see `README.md`). Each entry
notes which tag it feeds and, where it matters, which *direction* of injection it
shows: **W→C** (West injected into China) or **C→W** (Chinese injected into the West).

Compiled 2026-08-26 from Claude's knowledge — verify spellings/availability when
hunting images; add finds to the folder and log new sources in `README.md`.

## Photography — `space`, `neon`, `vhk`

- **Fan Ho (何藩)** — 1950s–60s HK street photography; geometric light/shadow,
  density + solitude. The canonical "compression" look. (`space`, `vhk`)
- **Michael Wolf** — *Architecture of Density*: HK facades as abstract pattern.
  Also *Hong Kong Informal Solutions* — back-alley improvisation. (`space`)
- **Greg Girard** — *City of Darkness* (Kowloon Walled City, with Ian Lambot);
  *Phantom Shanghai* — neon-lit interiors of both cities pre-demolition. (`neon`, `city`)
- **Romain Jacquet-Lagrèze** — *Vertical Horizon*, *The Blue Moment*, *Wild Concrete*
  (trees growing out of buildings — literally density vs. nature). (`space`)
- **Tommy Fung (@surrealhk)** — photoshopped surreal HK; a contemporary, playful,
  non-nostalgic way to use HK imagery. (`future`, `space`)
- **Ho Fan-adjacent counterweight:** shoot for *daylight* HK too — most references
  over-index on night neon. Rooftops, ferries, trails, podium gardens. (`space`)

## Graphic design & typography — `type`, `emw`, `vhk`

- **Henry Steiner** — Austrian-born "father of HK graphic design"; HSBC identity,
  Dairy Farm, HK Jockey Club. Literally the W→C injection in design form. (`emw` W→C)
- **Kan Tai-keung (靳埭強)** — ink calligraphy fused with modernist systems; Bank of
  China logo. The C-side synthesis master. (`emw`, `type`)
- **Alan Chan (陳幼堅)** — "East meets West" branding as an explicit practice:
  Mandarin Oriental, Mr Chan Tea Room packaging. (`emw`, `products`)
- **Stanley Wong / anothermountainman (又一山人)** — *redwhiteblue* series: the
  tricolor nylon tarp elevated to national-icon status. Vernacular → art. (`emw`, `products`)
- **Adonian Chan (陳濬人) / Trilingua Design** — revival of HK Beiwei calligraphy
  (北魏楷書), the brushy style of old shop signs; book *香港北魏真書*. The bridge
  between vintage signage and future type systems. (`type`, `future`)
- **M+ / NEONSIGNS.HK** — M+ museum's online neon-sign archive; **Tetra Neon
  Exchange** — NGO preserving decommissioned signs. Primary-source neon. (`neon`, `type`)
- **Yuefenpai (月份牌) / Zhiying Studio (杭穉英)** — 1920s–30s Shanghai calendar-girl
  posters: Western advertising form + lithography selling to Chinese consumers,
  qipao + Art Deco type. Dead-center W→C collision. (`city`, `emw`, `vhk`)
- **Chop-suey lettering** — the faux-brushstroke "Chinatown font" (C→W, invented in
  the West). Study it critically: it's the cliché the board should *transcend*,
  but its history maps the C→W injection precisely. (`emw` C→W, `type`)

## Illustration & comics — `future`, `emw`

- **Kongkee (江記)** — *Dragon's Delusion / Warring States Cyberpunk*: ancient-China
  souls in neon-psychedelic future HK. The single best "collision, projected
  forward" reference on this list. (`future`, `emw`)
- **Victo Ngai (倪傳婧)** — HK-born, NY-based; Chinese decorative density in Western
  editorial illustration (NYT, Apple). C→W. (`emw` C→W)
- **James Jean** — Taiwanese-American; Song-dynasty landscape logic in Western
  gallery/pop contexts (film posters for *Everything Everywhere All at Once*). (`emw` C→W)
- **Little Thunder (門小雷)** — HK comics/illustration; contemporary HK bodies and
  streetwear, zero nostalgia, very now. (`future`)

## Film & moving image — `neon`, `emw`, `future`

- **Wong Kar-wai + Christopher Doyle** — *In the Mood for Love* (the vintage palette:
  qipao, smoke, saturated interiors), *Chungking Express* (handheld neon present).
  An Australian cinematographer defining HK's look = W→C. (`vhk`, `neon`)
- **Ghost in the Shell (1995)** — Mamoru Oshii's city is explicitly modeled on
  HK (the canal montage). The template for "HK as the future." (`future`)
- **Blade Runner (1982)** — Chinatown-noir; C→W: Chinese signage as shorthand for
  the Western future. Interrogate it, then out-design it. (`future`, `emw` C→W)
- **Kung-fu exploitation posters (1970s)** — Bruce Lee-era posters drawn for Western
  audiences; lurid type + brush strokes. C→W at maximum volume. (`emw` C→W, `vhk`)
- **Love, Death & Robots: "The Witness"** — HK shot as saturated hyper-present,
  not nostalgia. (`future`, `neon`)

## Architecture — `space`, `emw`, `city`

- **HSBC Building (Norman Foster, 1985) vs. Bank of China Tower (I.M. Pei, 1990)** —
  facing pair: British high-tech in China vs. Chinese geometry in a Western
  skyscraper. The two injections literally staring at each other, with feng shui
  warfare between them. (`emw`, `space`)
- **I.M. Pei generally** — Suzhou Museum, Louvre pyramid: Chinese-born architect
  reshaping Western monuments. C→W. (`emw` C→W)
- **Tong lau (唐樓) & shophouses** — the ur-hybrid building type: Cantonese terraced
  form + colonial arcade. HK, Singapore (pastel Peranakan versions), Guangzhou. (`emw`, `city`)
- **Kowloon Walled City** — density mythology; source for half the games below.
  Girard/Lambot's *City of Darkness* is the primary document. (`space`)
- **The Bund + shikumen (石庫門), Shanghai** — Deco banks on the waterfront; stone-gate
  lane houses (Western row-house plan, Chinese courtyard logic). Xintiandi is the
  polished-retail version — useful and a cautionary tale at once. (`city`, `emw`)

## Fashion & streetwear — `emw`, `future`

- **Shanghai Tang** — David Tang's luxury reimagining of qipao/Tang jacket; the
  glossy commercial version of the thesis. (`emw`)
- **Vivienne Tam** — *Mao collection*, mesh cheongsam dresses in NY fashion. C→W. (`emw` C→W)
- **CLOT (Edison Chen) × Nike "Royale" silk Air Jordans/AJ1** — Chinese silk
  brocade on the most Western object possible. Product + fashion + streetwear
  in one reference. (`emw` C→W, `future`, `products`)
- **Samuel Guì Yang, Angel Chen** — contemporary London/Shanghai designers doing
  the collision without costume. Future-facing fashion refs. (`future`, `emw`)

## Products & packaging — `products` (use `emw` tag when saving)

- **Two Girls (雙妹嚜)** — HK's oldest cosmetics brand; calendar-girl art on Western
  toiletries. (`vhk`, `emw`)
- **Tiger Balm / White Flower Oil** — hexagonal jars, bilingual labels; medicine
  packaging as graphic identity. (`emw`, `products`)
- **Red A (紅A, Star Industrial)** — HK plastics: red tubs, lampshades over dai pai
  dongs. Vernacular industrial design. (`vhk`, `space`)
- **Camel & Vacuum flask brands** — mid-century HK thermos design; deco lines +
  Chinese florals. (`vhk`, `emw`)
- **G.O.D. (Goods of Desire)** — contemporary homeware remixing HK vernacular
  (letterbox prints, tenement motifs). The "how to use nostalgia without drowning"
  case study — sometimes it works, sometimes it's kitsch; both instructive. (`emw`, `future`)
- **Chinese takeout box & fortune cookie** — C→W objects invented in America;
  the pagoda-print oyster pail is pure invented-China graphics. (`emw` C→W)

## Games — `ui`, `future`

- **Sleeping Dogs (2012)** — still the benchmark HK open world; signage, wet
  streets, street food density. (`ui`, `neon`)
- **Stray (2022)** — Walled City-inspired verticality; warm-neon palette without
  human nostalgia. (`ui`, `future`, `space`)
- **Nine Sols (Red Candle, 2024)** — self-described "taopunk": Taoist cosmology ×
  sci-fi, gorgeous 2D art. Closest existing thing to "the collision, projected
  forward" in games. (`ui`, `future`)
- **Sifu (Sloclap, 2022)** — French studio, Pak Mei kung fu; W→C in game form,
  strong stylized-realism art direction. (`ui`)
- **ANNO: Mutationem / Loopmancer** — Chinese-made cyberpunk with HK-ish districts;
  uneven but useful palette refs. (`ui`, `future`)
- **Cyberpunk 2077 (Kabuki/Japantown)** — Asian-signage-as-future done at AAA
  scale; note it's the Japanese flavor — study the gap between that and a
  *Chinese* future aesthetic. (`ui`, `future`)
- **Mahjong Soul** — the incumbent's anime direction; the counter-reference: what
  this game should *not* look like, and proof the market lacks the HK-spirit slot. (`ui`)
- **Balatro** — nothing east-west about it; pure lesson in making a table card/tile
  game feel juicy and modern. (`ui`)

## Music — `emw`

- **Canto-pop as a form** — Western pop structures sung in Cantonese; the audio
  version of the whole thesis. Sam Hui for the vernacular 70s start; Anita Mui
  and Leslie Cheung for the glam 80s peak.
- **Faye Wong** — *Chungking Express*-era covers of the Cranberries and Cocteau
  Twins in Cantonese; a two-way artifact in a single track. (`emw`, `neon`)
- **Wu-Tang Clan** — kung-fu cinema mythology rebuilt into hip-hop. C→W, total
  absorption rather than quotation — the model for how deep the injection can go.
- **Shanghai jazz age (1930s–40s)** — Zhou Xuan, Paramount ballroom shidaiqu
  (時代曲): American jazz forms in Mandarin. The W→C injection on record, and the
  direct ancestor of Canto-pop. (`city`, `vhk`)

## Tokyo — parallel case study (`city`; method, not vocabulary)

Japan absorbed the West on its own terms and then injected *back* hard. Not the
Chinese collision, but the best-documented playbook for how absorption works —
study the mechanism, don't borrow the aesthetics wholesale.

- **Kurosawa two-way loop** — absorbed Ford westerns; *Seven Samurai* → *Magnificent
  Seven*, *Yojimbo* → *A Fistful of Dollars*. The cleanest documented case of
  inject-absorb-reinject in any medium.
- **City Pop & Shibuya-kei** — Western funk/AOR and 60s pop rebuilt as something
  unmistakably Japanese (Tatsuro Yamashita; Pizzicato Five, Cornelius). The music
  parallel to what the game's soundtrack could do with Canto sources.
- **Tadanori Yokoo** — poster art collaging ukiyo-e, pop art, and psychedelia;
  the graphic-design version of the absorption method. (`future`)
- **Akira / neo-Tokyo** — the East-Asian future-city template that *Ghost in the
  Shell* then relocated to HK. Useful to see what's Japanese about it so the
  Chinese version can differ. (`future`)
- **Metabolism architecture** — Nakagin Capsule Tower etc.: Japan proposing its
  own future instead of importing one. The ambition level to match. (`future`, `space`)
