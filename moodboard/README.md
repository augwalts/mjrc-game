# Moodboard — MJRC video game

Visual reference dump for the game's art direction. One flat folder — drop images
straight in here, no sorting required. This README is the index.

Everything except this README is gitignored (binaries stay local-only).

## Direction

**Copy the spirit of Hong Kong, not the look of Hong Kong.** HK's original energy
was a two-way injection: the West injected into China, and Chinese culture injected
back into the West. The board collects evidence of that *collision process* —
wherever it happened — not HK set dressing. A neon sign is only interesting here
if it shows the collision (English grotesque stacked on 繁體 calligraphy, a Western
product sold in a Chinese visual frame, a Chinese ritual restaged in a Western form).

So: east meets west, urban, multinational, multilingual. Guardrails:

1. **Don't drown in nostalgia.** Collect future-facing aesthetics too (neo-noir,
   Y2K revival, cyber-minimal, contemporary game UI) so the look reads as "the
   collision, projected forward," not a period piece.
2. **Don't collapse into HK cosplay.** Pull from every Chinese/Western hybrid city
   to isolate the shared language from any one city's clichés — Singapore and
   Shanghai to start (also fair game: Macau, Taipei, overseas Chinatowns).
   Tokyo is welcome too, as a *parallel* case: a hybrid culture that absorbed the
   West on its own terms, but Japanese rather than Chinese — borrow its *method*
   of absorption, not its vocabulary.

Spatial character: HK is uniquely urban *and* uniquely outdoors — extreme density
pressed against mountains, water, and open air. Life happens outside: street
markets, rooftop courts, podium gardens, dai pai dongs, harbourfront, trails
minutes from towers. Collect that compression/release rhythm, not just interiors
and signage.

## Themes (tags, not folders)

Use these as a mental checklist when saving, and optionally as filename prefixes:

- `vhk` — vintage Hong Kong: 60s–90s signage, cha chaan teng, streetscape, print ephemera
- `neon` — night city, wet streets, contemporary signage
- `emw` — **the core tag.** East/west collisions in either direction: Western forms
  in Chinese frames, Chinese forms in Western frames. Most visible domains:
  **architecture, graphics, fashion, products, food** (also ritual, music, film)
- `type` — multilingual lettering: 繁體, English, Japanese; layout systems, calligraphy vs. grotesque
- `future` — the anti-nostalgia tag: futurism, cyber-minimal, speculative HK
- `ui` — screenshots from other games worth stealing from
- `city` — comparative cities: Singapore, Shanghai, Macau, Taipei, Chinatowns.
  Optionally name the city in the desc, e.g. `city-shanghai-bund-deco.jpg`
- `space` — density + outdoor life: towers against mountains/water, street markets,
  rooftop courts, podium gardens, dai pai dongs, trails at the edge of the city

## Naming (optional, low effort)

`tag-shortdesc.ext` when you can be bothered, e.g. `neon-mongkok-rain.jpg`,
`ui-balatro-score-popup.png`. Unnamed phone dumps are fine — triage later.
