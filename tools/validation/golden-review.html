Golden-hand review sheet

121 hand-authored Hong Kong Old Style cases, 63 of them already known to split between houses. These are the answers the MJRC scoring engine will be held to. Every one is provisional until a strong HK player has signed it off — that sign-off is the exit requirement, and this sheet is how it gets done.

What you are being asked

  1. Read the tiles, the melds and the situation. Is the printed faan the faan your table would pay? That is the whole question.
  2. Mark 同意 / 不同意 / 存疑 on every case. An unmarked case counts as unvalidated, so "obviously right" still needs a tick.
  3. If you disagree, write the number you would pay and name the rule. "混一色 is 3 not 4 at my table" is worth more than "wrong".
  4. Cases flagged contested are ones we already know houses split on. Tell us which side you play — that is the point of the flag.
  5. A case marked refused claims the hand is under the 3-faan minimum and may not be taken at all. Check that as carefully as the scoring hands; it is the one nobody checks.
