Paper by default, charcoal on the toggle — the chrome follows the shell theme, the felt never changes. The HUD and modal CSS below is copied verbatim from client/gamepvp/index.html at HEAD (ids swapped for classes so several frames can sit on one page). The felt, tiles and the watch page are sketched, not the real renderer.

1 · the menu bar (HUD)

Panel strip with a hairline. Left: ruleset · round wind · speed in mono caps, then the game clock (elapsed since the first deal), then a seal-red reconnect chip when the socket drops. Right: two round icons only — chat (with its unread badge) and the gear. Pause, auto, settings and quit live in the gear's dropdown (open in the desktop frame); quit is the seal-red row. The bar sits above everything on the page, the veil included, so nothing ever paints over the icons.

2 · the quit dialog

The lab's modal: head with a hairline, body in dim ink, foot with one blue primary (stay — the default, also Esc) and one ghost (leave). The host sees two more blocks: remove a player (the other human seats; first tap arms the name in seal red, second tap removes) and end the table for everyone, its button in seal red. A bot plays the seat after leave; the seat and tiles are kept — rejoin from Home or the room page.

3 · /watch — the admin observer, four real screens

Admin only. /watch/:id opens the actual client four times, each attached as an observer of one seat: the same welcome, the same event stream, the same felt that player is looking at — their own hand at the bottom, the others as backs, the last discard ringed in seal red on their screen when it is their turn. Chrome is stripped (no gear, no chat), input is blocked, and a red watching chip in the HUD says so. Each cell is the client at 4:3; the grid is 2×2 on desktop, one column on a phone. ?panels=1 keeps the summary view from before. Tokens are minted per seat by the table and handed only to is_admin; an observer socket can send nothing but heartbeat and resync.

4 · the menu behind the gear — tabbed

Gear → one full menu, not a dropdown. Five tabs: game (pause, auto; host block: remove a player, end for everyone) · rules (the ruleset we are playing by, match length, the table's speed as a greyed slider) · table (tile size, double-tap on/off buttons, sort) · coach (the handicaps + beginner labels) · app (theme, language, sound, haptics, account). The footer is the same on every tab: leave the table in seal red on the left, done as the primary on the right, Esc = done. The head repeats the HUD title so you never lose where you are. Quit is no longer a separate menu item — leaving is always one tap away in the footer, and it still goes through the confirm dialog (section 2).

5 · new table — a role, and four empty human seats

The New-table modal gains one question at the top: your role. I play is today's behaviour — you take 東 and the other three are bots or open seats. I host only gives you no seat: all four seats start as open human seats (tap any to make it a bot), you land on the watch page with all four hands, and you keep the host controls — pause, remove a player, end the table — from there. Host-only tables are never rated, and a plate on every player's felt reads host: augie · watching, so nobody is observed without knowing. "Admin" stays what it is (is_admin, can watch any table); "teacher" is host-only plus, later, pause-and-ask — same role, more verbs. The rest of the modal is unchanged: name, rules and length (fixed when the room fixes them), speed, who can join.
