Gameplay lab — every screen after Play

A style sheet of the gamepvp table, restyled into the shell's language (the same tokens and components as lobby-lab.html and newtable-lab.html). Nothing here is wired to anything: it is a drawing of what the client would look like, screen by screen, in play order. The rule underneath all of it: the felt is content, everything else is chrome, and chrome follows the shell. The felt keeps its green and everything drawn on it keeps its dark plate in both themes, because the felt does not theme. Tiles are stand-ins — a rectangle and a glyph, not the real art.

Chrome — this theme

Set on the body, identical to the two shell labs. The table adds only rim (the felt's frame) and the on-felt constants below.

On the felt — constant

felt #12503F felt-dk #0D3D30 tile #F6F1E4 plate 8/20/15 .72

Plates, the state card, the call banner and the chat overlay sit on the felt, so they stay dark in light mode too. Only the rim and everything outside it flips.

What colour means

Blue — the one config colour: selected segments, switches, sliders, links, the primary button. Never on the felt.
Seal red #C1272D — act now: the ring on the tile in play, an unread dot, a seat still being waited on, disconnect.
Gold — twice only: the dealer's 莊 marker, and the win moment (the WIN button, the winning tile's ring).
Green — the felt (it is the table), and a gain in a number column. Red-pink — a loss in a number column.
Everything else is ink or dim. No emoji, no pastel fields, no side stripes.
