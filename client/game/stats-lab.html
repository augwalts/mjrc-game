Agreement by decision difficulty

How often the human played the engine's own top choice, split by how much better that choice was than its second.
The measured shape is a U, which is not what I expected. People play the obvious moves right (94%) and diverge on the close ones (29%) — and the near-tie band is half of every move graded. So the headline 49% understates them: agreement on decisions that actually cost something is 69%. I had assumed a raw match rate would flatter players by scoring forced moves as wins; on this data it does the opposite.
