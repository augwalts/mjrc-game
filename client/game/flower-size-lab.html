Flower legibility — the numeral and the name glyph

flower-lab.html laddered the index numeral and selected B2 · bottom-left · 52 on 2026-09-01. It never varied the name glyph — every rung on that ladder carries gl:{size:24} — and its own lock sheet lists that as still open: “the name glyph is untouched at 24 — still 9.6px in the palette, and more conspicuous once the numeral is fixed.” This page is that missing half.

It also adds a size that page does not have: the game draws flowers at --th:56px (40×56), and on the side seats they are now turned to face their player — so the glyph is read sideways at 56×40.

1 — the name glyph, at the sizes that actually ship

A CJK glyph needs roughly 13–14px of rendered height before the strokes separate. On a 140-tall canvas that means size 33 at the 40px palette, or size 24 at the 56px board — which is exactly today's value, and exactly why it reads on the board and dissolves in the palette.

2 — numeral: what ships now vs the selected B2

B2 also rescales the motif to 0.78 and shifts it (18, 2) to clear the corner. That part is not previewed here — the motif primitives are baked into each tile function in tile-engine.js and cannot be transformed without editing it. The numeral and glyph are patched live, which is what this page is for.
